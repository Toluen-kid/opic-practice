Tieu chi cham diem OPIC

OPIC danh gia dua tren 5 tieu chi chinh:

Task Completion
Tra loi day du cau hoi, bao gom du thong tin can thiet, khong lac chu de.
Fluency & Delivery
Noi tron tru, tu nhien, khong ngap nhieu, duy tri nhip do giao tiep.
Vocabulary
Dung tu phong phu, dung cho. Dung ngon ngu mo ta, so sanh, bieu cam.
Grammar
Dung da dang thi (present, past, conditional), cau phuc hop, it loi co ban.
Chien luoc dat IH-AL: (1) So sanh / Tuong phan (however, whereas) · (2) Nen / Khong nen (pros & cons) · (3) Dua ra quan diem + lap luan (In my opinion... because...) · (4) Li tuong vs thuc te (Ideally... but in reality...)
